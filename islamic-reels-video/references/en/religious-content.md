# Religious content: rules and checks

## What must never be depicted (no picture, no code, no silhouette)
- The Prophet ﷺ and other prophets, the Companions (Abu Salama, Aisha, etc.), angels, Allah — in no way.
  When they are mentioned: ornament, calligraphy, an empty room of the era, light, objects, nature.
- Angels — only as "light" (rays, radiance), with no figures, wings or beings.
- Under a mention of the Prophet ﷺ, never place random footage (feet, people, etc.) — only the card.

## People in pictures
- No faces: from behind, silhouettes, hands, feet, shadows. Never show a face, even partly.
- Women — in a full loose jilbab/khimar, nothing tight; men — in loose clothing.
- Modesty throughout: no revealing poses or exposed body (except feet/hands).
- The topic of death — with respect: never show the deceased, face or body; only the room, light, objects.

## Arabic text
- Ayat, hadith, duas, calligraphy are **typeset in code** (Amiri font) with diacritics and verified.
- Never take Arabic from an AI image and never generate an open mushaf with text — AI distorts the ayat.
  The mushaf — closed or with blurred pages; the ayah — as a separate panel in code.
- If the Arabic wording doesn't match the voiceover translation — show only the translation (no Arabic)
  to avoid a mismatch.

## Checking every ayah and hadith (before images)
1. Ayah: surah and number, the text per the mushaf, the translation matches the voiceover in meaning.
   If a word is omitted in the voiceover (e.g. "Say:" in 56:49), start the Arabic at the same place as the
   translation.
2. Hadith: collection and number (al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, at-Tirmidhi, Ahmad…), authenticity, the
   exact wording of key words. A long hadith — say that only part is quoted.
3. Numbers and science: the source (e.g. PRB — 117 billion people ever born; 8/117 ≈ 7 %;
   117e9 s ≈ 3,707 years).
4. Found a discrepancy — tell the user concretely: what the text says, what the source says, how to fix it
   (ElevenLabs can regenerate a single sentence; splicing it in is easy).

## Examples of verified sources (from past videos)
- 19:93–94 "He has enumerated them and counted them a [full] counting"; 56:49–50; 18:47; 19:95; 80:34–37;
  73:4 «وَرَتِّلِ الْقُرْآنَ تَرْتِيلًا».
- "People will be gathered barefoot and naked", Aisha's question — al-Bukhari 6527, Muslim 2859.
- "Every one of you will be spoken to by his Lord without an interpreter… protect yourselves from the Fire
  even with half a date" — al-Bukhari 7512, Muslim 1016.
- Abu Salama: "when the soul is taken, the sight follows it", "do not pray for yourselves except for good…",
  the dua «اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِأَبِي سَلَمَةَ…» — Muslim 920; "the sight follows the soul" — Muslim 921.
- The hadith of al-Bara' ibn 'Azib (angels with faces like the sun, a shroud and perfume from Paradise,
  "come out to forgiveness from Allah and His pleasure", the soul flows out like a drop from a waterskin) —
  Ahmad 18534, Abu Dawud 4753; the address there is «أَيَّتُهَا النَّفْسُ الطَّيِّبَةُ» ("O good soul");
  "O tranquil soul" is ayah 89:27.

## Formulas of respect
- After the Prophet's name — ﷺ (in the voiceover "sallallahu alayhi wa sallam"), in captions: "The Prophet ﷺ".
- Companions: رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ / عَنْهَا — as a small line by the label.
- "Amin" at the end of a dua can go on the final card.

## Sound
Only the voice, a nasheed without musical instruments (and without beats imitating instruments), or a
quiet ambience (wind, rain, birds). No instrumental music and no songs with instruments.
