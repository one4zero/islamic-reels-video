# Reels covers

## What performs (an analyzed example: "4.5 YEARS WITHOUT FOOD", 1.6M views)
- **One strong central image** with a glowing focal point (there — an octopus over glowing eggs).
- **A dark clean background**, at the top — a barely visible dot grid and one bright star (a "science/data" hint).
- **A big serif title in the lower third, 2 lines:**
  1) a number/fact — in a color with a glow (teal, gold);
  2) a short phrase — in cream, larger/bolder.
- **A small italic "hook"** under the title ("And that is only 1/100 of mercy") — intrigue, a second meaning.
- Contrast "warm object — cold glow".

## Geometry
- The frame is 1080×1920, but the Instagram profile grid shows the central 3:4 (y ≈ 240…1680) — everything
  important goes there.
- At the bottom of a grid tile there is a view counter (bottom-left ~8 % of the height) — keep the subtitle above it.
- The object — upper-middle (y ≈ 450…1150), the title — y ≈ 1150…1450.

## Title formulas
- **NUMBER + UNIT / SHORT FACT** + italic hook: "117 BILLION / IN ONE DAY" — "And each one alone".
- **The hook question** — if it is strong: "WHY DO THE EYES / STAY OPEN?" — "The Prophet ﷺ answered".
- No more than 5–6 words in two lines, no full stop at the end.

## Cover prompt (with text)
```
Vertical 9:16 Instagram Reels cover, dark minimal cinematic style. Background: deep black-navy with a subtle
grid of tiny faint dots in the upper part and one small bright golden star near the top. Center of the frame
(upper-middle area): <MAIN IMAGE — one glowing focal point>. Bottom third: a large centered title in an
elegant classic serif font, two lines: first line "<NUMBER/FACT>" in glowing warm gold with a soft light glow;
second line "<PHRASE>" in bold cream-white serif letters, slightly larger. Below them a small italic serif
subtitle in muted gold: "<HOOK>". All text is centered with wide margins and is not placed at the very
bottom edge. Photorealistic, high contrast, <series palette>, calm, solemn and powerful mood, modest Islamic
aesthetic. <faceless, seen from behind / no people>. No other text, no logo, no watermark.
```
Generators often garble non-Latin text and numbers → a version **without text** (drop the title/subtitle
lines) and put the title on in a Remotion Still (Lora 600 + glow, per the layout above).

## Cover checklist
- The title is readable in a small tile (check by shrinking to 300 px wide).
- Everything important is inside 3:4; the subtitle is not under the view counter.
- No faces, a sharia hijab, no distorted Arabic/Latin/Cyrillic text.
- The cover matches the first frame/hook of the video (promise = content).
